Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on pain, cost, time and what to expect. Still curious? Ask us on WhatsApp →

Does a root canal hurt?

Not at Dentalign. With rotary endodontics and profound anaesthesia, most single-sitting root canals feel like getting a filling — patients routinely return to work the same day. More on painless RCT →

How much does Invisalign cost in Goa?

It depends on complexity and duration. After a consultation and digital smile scan you receive an exact written quote. Message us on WhatsApp for a quick estimate.

How much do braces cost in Goa?

It depends on type (metal, ceramic, self-ligating) and complexity. After records and X-rays you get an exact written quote with staged payments. More on braces →

How long do braces take?

Typically 12–24 months. Mild cases can finish under a year; complex bites take longer. You'll get a month-wise plan at your first visit.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — costs for braces, aligners and implants can be staged across your treatment. Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll structure a plan that suits you.

What happens at the first visit?

A complete examination, X-ray if needed, a clear diagnosis in simple words, and a written plan with exact costs. Consultation is ₹500.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. Severe toothache, swelling, trauma or a broken tooth gets a same-day slot wherever possible — call +91 93229 72363.

Is teeth whitening safe for enamel?

Yes. We examine first, protect your gums during treatment, and use dentist-grade gels — nothing like abrasive online kits. More on whitening →

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Every 6 months for most people; every 3–4 months if you have gum disease, wear braces, or smoke/drink a lot of chai-coffee.

When should my child first see a dentist?

By age 1, or within 6 months of the first tooth. Early visits are short and fun — and prevent fear later. More on kids dentistry →
