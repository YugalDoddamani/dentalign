Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Does a root canal hurt?

Not at Dentalign. With modern rotary instruments and profound anaesthesia, most single-sitting root canals feel no different from a filling — patients routinely go back to work the same day.

How much does Invisalign cost in Goa?

It depends on complexity and duration. After a consultation and digital smile scan, Dr. Alissa shares an exact written quote. Message us on WhatsApp for a quick estimate.

How long do braces take?

Typically 12–24 months depending on alignment. Mild cases with clear aligners can finish sooner. You'll get a month-wise plan at your first visit.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — costs for braces, aligners and implants can be staged across your treatment. Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll structure a plan that suits you.

What happens at the first visit?

A complete examination, X-ray if needed, a clear diagnosis in simple words, and a written plan with exact costs. Consultation is ₹500.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. Severe toothache, swelling, trauma or a broken tooth gets a same-day slot wherever possible — call +91 93229 72363.
