Invisalign Clear Aligners in Goa

Straighten your teeth without wires or brackets — planned digitally, supervised by a Gold Medalist orthodontist.

An invisible way to a perfect smile

Invisalign uses a series of crystal-clear, removable aligners to gently move your teeth into place. No metal, no food restrictions, no awkward brace-face photos — most people won't even notice you're straightening your teeth. At Dentalign Miramar, your entire treatment is planned on a 3D model of your own teeth, so you see your projected result before you pay a rupee.

Your Invisalign journey, step by step

  1. Consultation & smile scan (Day 1). Examination, photos and a digital scan of your teeth. You discuss goals and timelines with Dr. Alissa.
  2. 3D treatment preview. We design your tooth movements digitally and show you the expected outcome — you approve it before we proceed.
  3. Aligner fitting. Your custom sets arrive; we fit the first, teach wear and care (20–22 hours/day), and schedule reviews.
  4. Reviews every 6–8 weeks. Quick progress checks; refinements included if any tooth needs extra guidance.
  5. Retainers & aftercare. Fixed + removable retainers lock in your result, with a clear maintenance plan.

Is Invisalign right for you?

Invisalign corrects crowding, gaps, overbite, underbite, crossbite and mild rotations in teens and adults. Severe skeletal discrepancies may need braces or orthognathic guidance instead — your scan and X-ray reveal the honest answer in one visit. Book your consultation and find out in 30 minutes.

Invisalign FAQs

How much does Invisalign cost in Goa?

It depends on complexity and the number of aligner sets. After your scan you get an exact written quote — WhatsApp us for a quick estimate.

How long does treatment take?

Mild cases: 6–9 months. Comprehensive corrections: 12–18 months. You'll see a projected timeline before committing.

Is Invisalign better than braces?

Aligners win on aesthetics, comfort and hygiene; braces win on price and some complex movements. Dr. Alissa recommends honestly after your scan.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Only mild tightness for a day or two per new set — far gentler than braces tightenings.
