Dental Implants in Panaji, Goa

Replace missing teeth permanently — eat, speak and smile like yourself again.

The closest thing to your natural tooth

A dental implant is a titanium root placed in the jawbone, topped with a crown that matches your own teeth. Unlike dentures, it doesn't slip or need removal; unlike bridges, it doesn't grind down healthy neighbouring teeth. You brush it, floss it and forget it — while it preserves your jawbone and facial structure for decades.

What implants can replace

  • Single missing tooth — one implant + crown, neighbours untouched
  • Multiple teeth — implant-supported bridges
  • Full arch — fixed teeth on 4–6 implants (no removable denture)
  • Loose dentures — implant-retained overdentures that click firmly in place

The implant journey

  1. Evaluation & 3D planning. Clinical exam plus X-ray/CBCT to map bone volume and nerve position precisely.
  2. Written plan & quote. Implant system, timeline and exact cost in writing — no surprises mid-treatment.
  3. Painless placement. Under local anaesthesia, typically 30–45 minutes per implant. Most patients work the next day.
  4. Healing (8–12 weeks). The implant fuses with bone (osseointegration). A temporary tooth keeps you smiling meanwhile.
  5. Crown fitting. A custom ceramic crown is fixed — shaped and shaded to blend invisibly.

Implants vs bridges vs dentures

Bridges sacrifice healthy tooth structure on either side; dentures accelerate bone loss and need periodic replacement. Implants cost more upfront but are usually the cheapest option over 15–20 years — and the only one that feels like your own tooth. We'll lay out all three options with honest pros, cons and costs, then let you decide.

Implant FAQs

How much do implants cost in Goa?

It depends on the system, bone condition and grafting needs. After evaluation you get an exact written quote — WhatsApp us for a range.

Is the surgery painful?

No — local anaesthesia keeps placement painless, and most patients need only routine medication for a day or two.

How long do implants last?

20+ years routinely, often a lifetime with good hygiene and check-ups. The crown may need replacement after 10–15 years.

Am I too old for implants?

Almost certainly not — bone quality matters, not age. Patients in their 70s–80s get implants successfully.
