Smile Designing & Veneers in Panaji

A smile designed for your face — previewed digitally before we touch a tooth.

Great smiles are designed, not accidental

Smile designing combines veneers, contouring, whitening and gum shaping into one harmonious plan — built around your face shape, lip line, skin tone and personality. Whether it's a wedding next month or confidence you've postponed for years, cosmetic dentist Dr. Jeff Joy shows you a digital preview of your new smile first, so there are no surprises — only delight.

What a smile makeover can fix

  • Stained, yellow or patchy teeth (whitening + veneers)
  • Chipped, cracked or worn edges
  • Gaps and slightly crooked front teeth
  • Uneven gum line ("gummy smile" correction)
  • Old, mismatched crowns and fillings
  • Short or "aged" looking teeth

How it works

  1. Smile consultation. Photos, videos and a discussion of what you love — and don't — about your smile.
  2. Digital smile preview. We design your new smile on screen and refine it with you until you love it.
  3. Trial smile. A temporary mock-up lets you "test drive" the shape and size in real life.
  4. Final veneers/crowns. Minimal-prep porcelain crafted to match — bonded in 1–2 visits.
  5. Aftercare. Polishing protocol, night guard if you grind, and review schedule for 10–15+ year life.

Porcelain veneers vs composite bonding

Porcelain offers superior stain resistance, translucency and longevity (10–15+ years) — ideal for full makeovers. Composite bonding is single-visit and budget-friendly, perfect for small chips and gaps. We'll recommend based on your teeth, timeline and budget, with both quotes in writing.

Smile designing FAQs

How much do veneers cost in Goa?

It depends on material and tooth count. After your smile analysis you get an exact written quote — WhatsApp us for a range.

Do veneers look fake?

Not ours. Each veneer is designed around your face — natural shade gradients and translucency, never flat white.

How long do veneers last?

Porcelain: 10–15+ years. Composite: 5–7 years. Check-ups and a night guard (for grinders) extend life.

Do veneers damage teeth?

Modern prep removes just 0.3–0.5 mm of enamel; many cases need almost no preparation at all.
