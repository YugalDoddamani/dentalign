Metal & Ceramic Braces in Goa

Time-tested teeth straightening — precise, affordable and more comfortable than ever.

Braces, perfected by a specialist

Modern braces are smaller, smoother and faster than the ones you remember. At Dentalign, every braces case is planned and supervised by Dr. Alissa Varella — Gold Medalist in Orthodontics — not delegated to juniors. That specialist precision is what finishes treatment on time, with a bite that functions as beautifully as it looks.

Choose your braces

  • Metal braces — the most affordable and durable; handles the most complex corrections
  • Ceramic (tooth-coloured) braces — blend with your teeth; favourite of working adults
  • Self-ligating braces — low-friction system, fewer visits, often faster alignment
  • Functional & growth appliances — for growing children with jaw development issues

Your braces journey

  1. Records & planning. Photos, X-rays and measurements; your month-wise plan and exact quote.
  2. Bonding day. Brackets placed in about an hour — completely painless — plus care kit and diet guidance.
  3. Monthly reviews. 20-minute adjustments tracking progress; most patients finish in 12–24 months.
  4. Debonding day. Braces off, teeth polished — the reveal you've waited for.
  5. Retention. Fixed + removable retainers protect your result for life, with annual reviews.

Braces for adults

Nearly half our orthodontic patients are adults — teachers, hospitality staff, brides-to-be and professionals. Ceramic braces and Invisalign keep treatment discreet, and evening slots (till 8 PM) mean no leave from work. It's never too late: healthy teeth move at any age.

Braces FAQs

How much do braces cost in Goa?

It depends on type and complexity. After records you get an exact written quote with staged payments — WhatsApp us for a range.

How long does treatment take?

Typically 12–24 months. Mild cases can finish under a year; you'll know your timeline at visit one.

Do braces hurt?

Only 2–3 days of mild soreness after fitting and adjustments — modern wires are far gentler than old ones.

Am I too old for braces?

No — healthy teeth move at any age. Many of our patients are working adults.
