Pediatric Dentistry in Panaji

Dental visits your child will actually look forward to — gentle, patient and fun.

First impressions last a lifetime

Most dental fear in adults traces back to one bad childhood visit. At Dentalign we do the opposite: tell-show-do techniques, short appointments, zero rushing and endless patience. Children explore the chair, "count" their teeth and leave proud — building a habit of easy dental care that protects them for decades.

Care for every growing smile

  • First visits & 6-month check-ups — fun, fear-free, over before boredom sets in
  • Tooth-coloured fillings — painless treatment for cavities in milk and permanent teeth
  • Fluoride & sealants — invisible shields that prevent most childhood cavities
  • Habit correction — thumb-sucking, tongue-thrusting and mouth-breathing guidance
  • Early orthodontic screening (by age 7) — catch jaw issues while growth can fix them
  • Sports mouthguards — custom protection for football, cricket and cycling

A typical first visit

  1. Meet & play. Your child explores the clinic and chair at their own pace — no instruments yet.
  2. Count the teeth. A gentle check-up framed as an adventure, with parents beside them throughout.
  3. Cleaning (if ready). A tickly polish — only if your child is comfortable. Never forced.
  4. Parent coaching. Brushing technique, diet traps (juice boxes!) and a recall plan you can follow.

Parents ask us most…

"Do milk teeth really matter?" — Absolutely. They hold space for permanent teeth, guide speech and chewing, and decayed milk teeth can infect developing adult teeth below. A small filling today prevents pain, swelling and bigger bills tomorrow.

Kids dentistry FAQs

When should my child first see a dentist?

By age 1, or within 6 months of the first tooth. Early visits are short and fun — they prevent fear later.

Do milk-teeth cavities need treatment?

Yes — untreated decay causes pain and can damage permanent teeth below. Small cavities need only simple, painless fillings.

What is fluoride application?

A quick, painless mineral coating that strengthens enamel — recommended 6-monthly for cavity-prone kids.

When should we think about braces?

Screening by age 7. Some jaw issues are far easier and cheaper to correct while your child is growing.
