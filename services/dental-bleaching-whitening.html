Teeth Whitening, Cleaning & Polishing

Erase years of chai, coffee and tobacco stains — safely, in about an hour.

A brighter smile, the safe way

Over-the-counter whitening kits and abrasive "charcoal" powders promise miracles but often damage enamel and irritate gums. Professional treatment at Dentalign starts with a proper examination — we check for decay, cracks and gum disease first — then combine ultrasonic scaling, stain removal, polishing and dentist-grade whitening gel for a result that's dramatically brighter yet completely enamel-safe.

What's included

  • Ultrasonic scaling — tartar and plaque removed above and below the gumline
  • Stain removal & polishing — chai, coffee, wine and tobacco stains buffed away
  • Professional whitening — dentist-grade gel lightens enamel several shades in one sitting
  • Gum health check — bleeding or recession flagged early, with a home-care plan

What to expect

  1. Check-up first. We confirm your teeth and gums are healthy enough for whitening.
  2. Cleaning & polish (30–40 min). Scaling plus stain removal — many patients stop here, delighted.
  3. Whitening (optional, ~45 min). Gums protected, gel applied in cycles, shade checked together.
  4. Aftercare guidance. 48-hour "white diet" tips and maintenance plan for 1–3 year results.

Keep it bright

Whitening typically lasts 1–3 years depending on lifestyle. A 6-monthly cleaning + polish maintains both brightness and gum health — our most popular routine visit. Planning a wedding or event? Book whitening 2–3 weeks prior for the perfect finish.

Whitening FAQs

Is whitening safe for enamel?

Yes. We examine first, protect your gums during treatment, and use dentist-grade gels — nothing like abrasive online kits.

How long does it last?

Typically 1–3 years. Yearly cleaning + polish keeps brightness topped up.

How much does cleaning cost?

A standard scaling + polish is quick and affordable — WhatsApp us for the exact price and same-week slots.

Will it whiten crowns or veneers?

No — whitening works on natural enamel only. We always whiten before shade-matching new restorations.
