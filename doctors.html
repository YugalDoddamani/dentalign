Meet your specialists

At Dentalign you are always treated by a postgraduate specialist — 25+ years of combined experience, zero hand-offs.

Chief Orthodontist

Dr. Alissa Varella

B.D.S., M.D.S. Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics · Invisalign Certified (USA)

A perfectionist with a keen eye for detail, Dr. Alissa believes orthodontics is one of the most intricate forms of art — and that changing a patient's smile changes their life. Her expertise spans malaligned teeth, dentofacial reconstruction, growth modification with functional appliances, and treatment of cleft lip & palate and complex syndromes.

  • Gold Medalist in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics
  • AAO Charley Schultz Resident Scholar Award — American Association of Orthodontists Annual Session, Washington D.C.
  • Dr. V.P. Jayade Award & Bite In Award for Excellence in Orthodontics
  • Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) Grant recipient
  • Published research author — American Journal of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics, plus national & international journals
  • International Member — American Association of Orthodontists, World Federation of Orthodontists & Indian Orthodontic Society

Chief Endodontist & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Jeff Joy

B.D.S., M.D.S. Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics

Dynamic and innovative, Dr. Jeff has one mission: to end the myth that root canals are painful. He blends patient education with rotary endodontic technology so thoroughly that his patients "hardly ever notice they're getting a root canal done." His superpower? Saving teeth through RCT that others would extract.

  • Painless single-sitting root canals — simple to highly complex anatomies
  • Smile designing — veneers, laminates, crowns & full-mouth rehabilitation
  • Tooth preservation — rebuilding badly broken, decayed or traumatised teeth
  • Surgical endodontics — periapical cysts, trauma cases & retreatments
  • Tooth-coloured fillings — invisible, mercury-free restorations
